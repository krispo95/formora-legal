Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 June 2026 · Русский · Español

This Privacy Policy explains how Formora ("we", "the app") handles your data when you use the iOS application. We designed Formora to keep your health information on your device whenever possible; this document tells you exactly what we collect, where it is stored, and what you can do about it.

1. Who runs the app

Formora is an independently developed iOS application. If you need to reach us, write to pogosyan.chris@gmail.com.

2. What data the app handles

2.1 Measurements you enter

The body measurements you record yourself — weight, body fat, lean mass, circumferences (chest, waist, hips, thigh, etc.), height, and any custom measurements you add — are saved in the app's local database on your iPhone. We do not upload them to any server.

2.2 Progress photos

Photos you take or import as progress photos are stored in the app's private container on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere, are not shared with us, and are deleted when you delete the photo from the app or uninstall it.

2.3 Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you turn on Apple Health sync in Settings, Formora reads weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass, height, and waist circumference samples from Apple Health, and writes the matching measurements you save in Formora back to Apple Health. HealthKit data is exchanged directly between the app and the iOS Health database — it never leaves your device through Formora. You can revoke read or write access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Formora.

2.4 Profile data

You may optionally enter a name, gender, date of birth, and height so the app can compute BMI, body fat, and lean mass. These values are stored locally and never transmitted.

2.5 Subscriptions (PRO)

If you purchase a PRO subscription, the purchase is processed by Apple's App Store using the Apple ID associated with your device. We rely on RevenueCat (a third-party service operated by RevenueCat Inc.) to validate purchases and check subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous device identifier and the Apple transaction receipt for that purpose — no personal information, no measurements, and no photos. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

2.6 Diagnostic data

If a crash occurs, an anonymised crash report (technical details such as the file name and line where the crash happened, iOS version, and device model) may be collected to help us fix the issue. These reports contain no measurements, photos, or personally identifiable information. Crash reporting can be disabled in the app's Settings.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information by default.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics that profile users.

4. How long data is kept

Your measurements, photos, and profile data stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Subscription receipts are kept by Apple and RevenueCat for the lifetime of the subscription as required for billing and compliance.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete all data Formora has saved from Settings → Data Management → Reset all data. This removes every measurement, photo, and preference from your device. Uninstalling the app also removes its data container, including the SwiftData database and stored photos.

6. Data security

Data is stored in the app's private container, protected by iOS file-system encryption when your device is locked. Because data stays on-device, the security of your information depends on the security of your device — we recommend using a strong passcode and Face ID / Touch ID.

7. Children

Formora is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has used the app and entered data, please contact us so we can advise.

8. Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you control it directly: view it in the app, edit any record, export everything to CSV/PDF (PRO), or delete it at any time. If you live in the EU/EEA, the UK, or California, you may also email us to ask what diagnostic data tied to your device (if any) we hold; we will reply within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be highlighted in the app's release notes.

10. Contact

For privacy questions: pogosyan.chris@gmail.com.
